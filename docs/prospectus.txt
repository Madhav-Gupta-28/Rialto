RIALTO DEMO SENIOR NOTE 2027
Acme Infrastructure Holdings Limited
ISIN GB00RIALTO00

STATUS AND RANKING
The Notes are senior secured obligations of the Issuer. They rank ahead of all
present and future unsecured and subordinated indebtedness of the Issuer, and
pari passu with all other senior secured obligations.

PRINCIPAL TERMS
Issuer            Acme Infrastructure Holdings Limited, incorporated in England
Nominal value     GBP 100 per Note
Aggregate size    GBP 10,000,000
Issue date        1 September 2026
Maturity date     1 September 2027
Redemption        At par on the maturity date

SECURITY
The Notes are secured by a first-ranking fixed charge over the Issuer's
contracted revenue from three operating assets, and by a floating charge over
the Issuer's remaining undertakings.

COVENANTS
The Issuer shall not incur additional secured indebtedness ranking ahead of the
Notes. Net leverage shall not exceed 3.5x EBITDA, tested semi-annually.

RISK FACTORS
The Notes are not listed and no secondary market is expected to develop. The
Issuer's revenue is concentrated in three counterparties. Recovery on default
depends on the realisable value of the charged assets, which is not guaranteed.

This document is a demonstration instrument prepared for the Rialto project.
It is not an offer of securities and describes no real company.
